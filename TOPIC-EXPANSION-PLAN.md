# GCSEMathsAI — Topic Expansion Plan

## Competitive Audit: Third Space Learning vs GCSEMathsAI

### Why expand?

Third Space Learning (TSL) has **450+ individual topic pages** where GCSEMathsAI has **73**. Each TSL page targets a specific long-tail search keyword (e.g. "area of a trapezium GCSE"), generating organic traffic from students searching for help on that exact subtopic. GCSEMathsAI bundles subtopics into broad pages (e.g. "Area of 2D Shapes"), which means we miss hundreds of specific search queries.

### The Strategy

Break each existing broad topic into **granular subtopic pages**, each with:
- Step-by-step method explanation
- 2–3 worked examples
- Common mistakes / exam tips
- Link to AI practice on that subtopic
- Free downloadable worksheet (PDF) — Phase 2

### What TSL Offers Per Topic (our target format)
- Step-by-step revision guide
- Worked examples with diagrams
- Practice questions (skill-based + applied + exam-style)
- Free downloadable worksheet PDF with mark scheme
- Diagnostic questions (20 MCQs to catch misconceptions)
- Revision mat (single-page visual summary)
